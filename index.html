Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Registrar Management Information System ("RMIS", "we", "our", or "the System"), operated for Saint Joseph's College of Baggao, uses the Gmail API to communicate with applicants and students.

What the System Does

RMIS is an internal admissions and student records tool used by the Registrar's Office. It sends automated email notifications related to:

  • Admission application approval, including new student account credentials
  • Admission application denial notices
  • Password reset One-Time Passwords (OTPs)

Gmail API Use and Scope

RMIS connects to a single Google account belonging to the Registrar's Office using the Gmail API, requesting only the following OAuth scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send — permission to send email on behalf of the authorized Registrar account.

This scope grants send-only access. RMIS does not request, and cannot use, any scope that would allow it to read, search, modify, or delete messages in the Gmail inbox, contacts, or any other Google account data. The Registrar account is used solely as the sending address for outgoing notifications generated by the System.

What Data Is Sent

Emails sent through this integration may include an applicant's or student's name, student ID, generated login credentials, admission decision status, and password-reset codes. This information is entered into RMIS directly by Registrar staff or generated by the System itself, and is transmitted only to the intended recipient's email address as provided during the admission or account process.

Data Storage and Security

The OAuth credentials used to authorize sending are stored locally on the Registrar's Office server and are not shared with any third party. RMIS does not sell, rent, or share any personal data collected through this system with external organizations for marketing or advertising purposes.

Data Retention

Email content is generated at send time and is not separately archived by RMIS beyond what is necessary for delivery. Records related to admission and student accounts are retained in accordance with the School's internal records-retention policies.

Revoking Access

The Registrar account holder may revoke RMIS's access to their Google account at any time via Google Account Permissions. Revoking access will stop all outgoing email notifications from the System until access is re-authorized.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or how RMIS uses Google account data can be directed to the Registrar's Office of Saint Joseph's College of Baggao.

This policy applies specifically to RMIS's use of Google's Gmail API under Google's API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
